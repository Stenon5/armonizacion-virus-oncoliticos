## What attribution means here

If you reuse the data, cite this repository using
[`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff) and keep the `source_doi` and
`source_location` columns intact. Those columns are what makes a value
checkable against the paper it came from. A copy of the table without them is
not a usable dataset.

## Third party material

The papers listed in [`docs/sources.md`](docs/sources.md) have their own
licenses, recorded in that file. No PDF, figure or complete supplementary
table from those papers is redistributed here. Individual extracted values are
stored with their citation and their location inside the source.

If you need the original article, follow its DOI and use it under its own
license.
